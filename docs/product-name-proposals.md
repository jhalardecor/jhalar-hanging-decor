## Design-family grouping

Recommendation: 13 new series proposals for the 19 remaining images, plus one possible match to an existing Shobha variant. A family is based on construction, arrangement and terminal details—not just colour. Different-looking white strands are deliberately kept separate.

| Proposed series | Source numbers | Structural basis visible in the photos |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pushpika Series | 0015 | Repeated capped pom-poms on a bead strand, ending in a flower-shaped pendant rather than a bell. |
| Kalash Series | 0016 | Kalash-like vessel motifs and small pom-poms on a bead strand, with a bell finish. |
| Gulab Series | 0017, 0018 | Open rose forms with paired leaves along an exposed bead chain, ending in a bell. |
| Champa Series | 0019 | Spaced, elongated trumpet-shaped floral elements on a slender strand, with a small flower at the end. |
| Mallika Series | 0020 | A continuous, dense bud-cluster strand with a decorative bell-shaped terminal. |
| Chandrika Series | 0021, 0024, 0028, 0034 | Three circular, spoked gota-style medallions, pom-poms, bead connectors and a bell. |
| Manjari Series | 0022, 0029 | Bud/bead clusters alternating with pom-pom groups on a strand, with a bell finish. |
| Latika Series | 0023, 0025 | A short vertical floral body with leaf collars, pom-pom tiers and three lower pom-pom drops. Confirm that the two central floral textures belong to one family. |
| Kanak Series | 0026 | A fine decorative bead string with small repeating motifs, without the larger flowers or pom-poms of the other families. |
| Vallari Series | 0030 | Slender bud/bead sections interrupted by small rosebud groups, with a bell finish. |
| Vasanti Series | 0031 | Dense bud panels separated by open roses and leaf collars, with an ornamental terminal. |
| Jharokha Series | 0032 | Square ornamental frames alternating with small pom-poms on a bead strand, with a bell. |
| Ratnavali Series | 0033 | A bead strand with larger decorative spherical ornaments, textile bands and a square ceremonial-bell-style terminal. |

### Products_0027 — do not invent another identity

`Products_0027_39bd2a25-dec1-44ec-bd7e-736abd8bd039.jpg` visually resembles the already labelled **Shobha Series – Red Yellow & White** image: alternating floral colours, small contrasting star-like flowers and hanging floral drops. The suggested display form uses the protocol’s em dash: **Shobha Series — Red Yellow & White**.

The owner subsequently chose **Keep it separate**. JH-027 and JH-035 remain distinct catalogue entries with the same canonical name and different source images. Their visible references disambiguate enquiries; no size or design difference is invented.

## Colour-name approach

Reuse these labels already visible in the reference filenames where they appear suitable: **Rani Pink**, **Golden Marigold**, **Pearl White**, **Kesari Bloom**, **Saffron Green**, and **Red Yellow & White**. Reuse in this proposal does not establish an approved series/colour pairing.

The following additional colour labels are proposals, with their intended palette notes:

| Proposed colour | Intended palette, subject to reference/swatches | Source numbers |

Colour judgements are approximate visual observations, not calibrated swatch matches. “Pearl” and “Gold” in these draft colour names refer to appearance, not verified pearl or gold materials. Botanical-sounding series names are brand-name proposals, not botanical or material certifications.
