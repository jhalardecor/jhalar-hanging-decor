JHALAR Logo Motion Concepts

Three distinct directions for the footer logo, all brand-true (festive, premium, elegant — never random). Watch each loop at least once, then pick a winner — or mix elements. The winner becomes the site footer. All three freeze to a clean static logo under reduced-motion settings.

Concept 1

Minimal Premium

The sign hangs; only light breathes. One slow champagne wave crosses every 8 seconds with a whisper of a glow — expensive silence. Nothing ever jumps, pops, or flashes.

Cycle
8s — slowest, calmest
Layers
Light wave · whisper breath · caption glow
Best for
Maximum luxury restraint; pairs with a quiet premium page

Concept 2

Dynamic Modern

Festival night: a fast golden wave with a spark leader races through every 3 seconds, ornaments pop left-right like dancing flames, one sparkler glint fires per cycle. Highest energy of the three — impossible to miss.

Cycle
3s — fastest, liveliest
Layers
Wave + spark · flame pops · sparkler glint · diya breath
Best for
Maximum engagement and festive energy

Concept 3 · ✓ selected — now live in the footer

Cinematic

A 7-second mini-film on loop: darkness, letters assembling from blur, a light ignition with a slow push-in, then a long glory hold. The closest to a true video-logo bumper.

Cycle
7s repeating film: darkness → assembly → ignition → hold
Layers
Repeating assembly · ignition wave + flash + glint · push-in · hold
Best for
Drama and memorability; most like a brand ident

Winner: Concept 3 — Cinematic, now live in the site footer.
