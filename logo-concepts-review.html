Logo motion concepts

Three directions for the footer logo. Each plays live below. Concept 3 now runs in the footer.

Concept 1 · Minimal Premium

The sign hangs and the light moves. One champagne wave crosses every 8 seconds with a soft glow. Nothing jumps, pops or flashes.

Cycle
8 seconds, the slowest loop
Layers
Light wave, soft breath, caption glow
Fits
Pages that need the logo to stay quiet

Concept 2 · Dynamic Modern

Festival night. A gold wave with a spark leader crosses every 3 seconds. Ornaments answer left to right in sequence. One glint fires per cycle.

Cycle
3 seconds, the fastest loop
Layers
Wave and spark, flame pops, glint, diya breath
Fits
The highest-energy read of the three

Concept 3 · Cinematic

The sign never rests. It sways toward and away in depth while charms swing in a breeze wave and letters bob in sequence. No flashing, no blinking.

Cycle
Eternal loop: 7s sway, 3s wave, 2.6s swing
Layers
3D sway, breeze swing, letter wave, embers
Fits
Motion that never rests
