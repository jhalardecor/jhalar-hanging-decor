Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. How This Website Works

This is a static website. It has no server-side database and no backend that receives form submissions. The enquiry form on our home page does not send anything to us on its own: when you press the button, your browser builds a WhatsApp message from what you typed and opens WhatsApp so you can review it. Nothing is transmitted until you press send inside WhatsApp, and nothing you type is stored on this website.

If you close the form without sending the WhatsApp message, we never receive your details at all.

2. Information We Receive

We only receive information you actively send us, through WhatsApp, a phone call or email. Depending on what you choose to include, that may be:

  • Your name and the contact number or email you message us from
  • Your business or organisation name, if you give it
  • What you are looking for: design, colours, quantity and the date you need it
  • Anything else you write in your message

The email field on the form is optional. If you leave it blank, we simply will not have it.

3. How We Use It

We use what you send us to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quotation, and arrange and deliver your order. We do not use it for anything else, and we do not send marketing messages to people who have only made an enquiry.

4. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade or rent your information.

Two third parties are involved in the normal running of this site:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host, its servers record standard technical request logs.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries any message you choose to send us. Those messages are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy, not this one.

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what information about you we hold
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • Ask us to delete your enquiry and message history
  • Ask us to stop contacting you
